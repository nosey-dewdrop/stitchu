2) DESCRIPTION (EN, musteri kopyasi)
------------------------------------------------------------------
A-line mini shift dress: square neckline, spaghetti straps, clean A-line fall.

This is not a standard-size pattern. The pattern is drafted to the
measurements you provide: bust, waist, hip, shoulder, back length and neck
(in cm), plus your chosen dress length (300 to 480 mm, mini to above-knee).
A deterministic pattern-making engine recomputes every piece from those
numbers. The pieces are drafted, not graded and not scaled from a base size.

WHAT THE FILE CONTAINS?
- Print-ready PDF tiled to A4 sheets, with corner codes (A1 top left) and
  edge ticks, so the sheets tape together edge to edge without guessing.
- A cover page with a numbered cutting list: every piece, its cut
  instruction, and the exact sheets it prints on.
- A calibration block: a 3 cm square and a 1 inch bar. Print at 100% scale
  and verify before cutting.
- 15 mm seam allowance included: cut on the outer line, sew on the inner
  fine line.
- Pieces: Top Front, Top Back, bias binding (neckline + armholes),
  spaghetti straps.
- A fabric estimate computed for your body (the EU38 default reads 2.15 m
  at 140 cm width).
- A true-scale SVG (1 unit = 1 mm) for plotter or projector cutting.

WHY MADE TO MEASURE?
A standard pattern is drafted once for a size chart, and your body is asked
to fit it. Here the direction is reversed: your measurements drive the
drafting formulas, so the square neck sits where your shoulder actually is
and the length is the length you asked for. Two different bodies get two
visibly different sets of pieces from the same design.

SKILL LEVEL?
Confident beginner and up. Straight seams, bias-bound neckline and armholes,
thin straps. Every sheet is numbered and the cover tells you where each
piece prints.

Measurements are processed in the browser when using the live studio; they
never leave your device.

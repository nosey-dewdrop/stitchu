# stitchu blog — patterns journal (2026-07-17)

Sequential log of building the blog you asked for (more than once) and never got: a
real journal page that gathers the 60s/70s vintage collection AND every sewable
pattern into one browsable place.

## What I built

1. **Read the site to match its style exactly.** patterns/index.html, showcase.html,
   collection-60s70s.html, index.html, the shared header (js/shared-header.js), and the
   header-diff + style-lint gates. Copied the patterns/index.html card grid conventions
   1:1 (same tokens, hairline borders, Didot headings, navy on white).

2. **New page: web/blog/index.html** — "The Pattern Journal." Generated from one source
   (engine/tools/gen-blog.mjs) off web/patterns/svg/meta.json + the approved EN/TR card
   copy already in patterns/index.html. Two sections:
   - **The Sixties Seventies Collection.** — intro + a card that links straight to the
     existing collection-60s70s.html (Quant, Biba, Courrèges, Pucci and more, 12 vintage
     looks). I did not duplicate the vintage content, I link to the page that already
     holds it, so there is one source of truth.
   - **Patterns From The Benchmark.** — all 12 sewable patterns from meta.json, each a
     card with: the engine's rendered SVG thumbnail, Title-Cased style name, garment
     type, piece count, fabric metrage, and the honest "Drawn by ... added in patch X"
     attribution. Each card links to its existing web/patterns/<slug>.html detail page.
   - Honest framing kept: "37 of 54 real product photos turn into a full pattern, and
     counting", with the misses pointed to the benchmark and patch notes.

3. **Nav wired everywhere.** Added "blog" / "günlük" to the canonical header on all 47
   pages (9 main + 24 styles + 13 patterns + the blog page itself), placed right after
   "patterns" so content sits together. header-diff.mjs was extended to normalise the
   blog self-link (like it already does for the patterns link) and to scan web/blog/;
   it reports the header byte-identical across all 47 pages.

## Design-rule compliance

- **Title Case** on every heading, section title, nav label, card title, and CTA
  ("The Pattern Journal.", "Patterns From The Benchmark.", "Draft One To Your
  Measurements, Free."). Verified by eye and grep.
- **No em dash** anywhere on the page (grep count 0).
- **No purple links** — every anchor I added has an explicit colour (var(--navy) for
  crumbs, counters, section links, honest-line links, footer; card colour navy). No
  browser-default anchor.
- **EN + TR on every string**: 100 data-en / 100 data-tr, exact parity. Proper TDK
  Turkish, natural capitalisation and punctuation.
- Aesthetic: quiet navy #1f3a5f on white, hairline borders, small type, no emoji, no
  gradient, no pill, no colored single word, no cream, no single-edge accent bar.
- No lorem / TODO / placeholder text.

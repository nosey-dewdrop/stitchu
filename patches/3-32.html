3.32

The yoke can gather now: a babydoll dress where the lower body is cut wider and gathers into the yoke seam for real fullness, not just a plain yoke over an A-line skirt.

2026-07-23
  • The plain yoke shipped a few patches ago, but a real doll or babydoll dress gathers fabric into the yoke, and the plain yoke could not do that. A gathered yoke variant now cuts the front and back body pieces wider at their top edge by a 2 to 1 ratio, the classic babydoll fullness, read from the same contract table the shirred bodice uses rather than an invented number. Gather notches and a cut note tell the sewer to gather the body edge to fit the yoke. The truing is the point: the body top edge is physically twice as wide, 432.9mm against the yoke's 216.5mm, but its sewn length gathers back down to match the yoke seam exactly, so the pieces still sew together.
  • The plain yoke path stays unchanged under golden_check, the golden reference is untouched, all 52 tests pass, and a gathered-yoke dress with a Peter-Pan collar drafts with no issue in that run's validator log. Honest limit: the 2 to 1 ratio and the flare shape are a sensible babydoll default, not fit-proven; how the fullness actually swings is a call for a sewn sample.

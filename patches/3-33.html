3.33

The first localized fullness: a center inverted box pleat. A swing top can now hang a single crisp fold at center front instead of spreading fullness evenly like a gather.

2026-07-23
  • Until now the engine could only add fullness by gathering, spread evenly across an edge. A swing or doll top often wants a single inverted box pleat at center front instead: the fabric is cut wider at the center and folds into one crisp pleat, not a hundred little gathers. This is the engine's first localized fullness primitive. The center front is cut wider by the pleat underlay, 40mm deep each side per the Aldrich box-pleat range, and fold lines plus a fold-to-center marking are stamped. The truing: the flat piece is 80mm wider at the center, 324.4mm against the plain 244.4mm, and when the pleat is folded out the finished width returns to 244.4mm exactly, zero drift, so it still sews to the yoke and the side seams still match.
  • It composes with the yoke, so a yoke swing top drafts with a box pleat hanging from the yoke, no issue in that run's validator log. Golden diff unchanged (golden_check) when off, all 53 tests pass. Honest limits, on the record: the pleat swings free the full length (a top-stitched-down swing pleat is not modeled yet), and it presses to the center fold, so it honestly refuses on an opened-front placket or a skirt with no center panel rather than drafting something that will not fold.

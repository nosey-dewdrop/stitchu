3.35

The bottleneck fix: your photo can now actually reach the cup seam, the yoke and the box pleat. The engine could draw them, but the photo-to-pattern bridge never sent them to the engine, so it kept saying it could not draw them.

2026-07-24
  • Six patches added real construction to the engine: the cup seam, the plain and gathered yoke, the center box pleat. But the live photo path did not wire them through. The vision layer read a bustier or a yoke from your photo, and the honesty layer then told you the engine could not draw it, while the engine sat there able to draw it. The real gap was one layer deeper than it looked: the function that hands a spec to the drafting engine never passed these three fields across the boundary at all, so even a correct spec was ignored. That boundary now carries cup seam, yoke and box pleat, the bridge maps a bustier photo to a cup seam and a doll or babydoll photo to a plain or gathered yoke, and the honesty layer only speaks up for the cases the engine genuinely still refuses.
  • The bridge mirrors the engine's own rules exactly, so it never sends a spec the engine will refuse and leave you with nothing: a cup seam is wired only for a strapless princess bustier with a sweetheart, square or scoop neckline, a yoke only for a dress or top with a bodice. Verified end to end: a strapless sweetheart photo-spec drafts four cup pieces with zero issues, a babydoll yoke photo-spec drafts the yoke pieces, and a sleeved or non-bustier read correctly stays in the honesty layer. Box pleat has no structured vision field yet, so it reads from the free-text channel only, an honest gap noted for a later vision-schema pass. Engine and golden untouched, this is bridge wiring.

3.29

A yoke seam, the first of the new capabilities after the v1.0 freeze: an opt-in horizontal split that turns the bodice into a yoke and a lower body, the doll and swing dress construction.

2026-07-23
  • With v1.0 frozen, this is one of the first new capabilities of the v1.1 line. An opt-in yoke flag splits the front and back into a Front Yoke, a Back Yoke, a Front Body and a Back Body, so a dress can hang from a fitted yoke the way a babydoll or swing dress does. The yoke line is measured at 28 percent of the panel's shoulder-to-hem drop, so the seam sits high on the chest and back where the doll silhouette wants it.
  • Proven the same way the freeze was: the golden reference stays byte-identical when the yoke is off, all 52 engine tests pass, and the split drafts wearable with the yoke and lower-body seams matched to sew. The honest limit, on the record: this is a plain yoke. The lower body does not gather yet, so the swing comes from the A-line skirt block rather than from fullness added at the yoke; a gathered yoke is a later capability.

3.8

Another AI measured my pattern and found a real bug. I fixed it.

2026-07-17
  • An independent language model measured one of the engine's A4 patterns, a sleeveless boat-neck button blouse, as vectors and caught a genuine mistake: the front was drawn with a button placket AND cut on the fold at the same time. You cannot sew buttons onto a fold, so that piece could never open and never go over the head. It was unwearable. A placket opens the garment at the center front, and cutting on the fold closes it, the two decisions contradicted each other and the engine let both through. It is fixed now: whenever the engine draws a front placket, the front becomes two mirror fronts that open at the center, its neck facing follows, and the button stand grows correctly past the center front on every front shape, not just some. The boat neckline was also too narrow, reading like a wide round neck; it now opens wide and shallow toward the shoulders the way a real bateau does. And a sleeveless garment now counts the bias binding that finishes its armholes.
placket fronts now wearable · boat neck opens 226 mm wide · armhole finish counted

This is a correctness fix, not a new feature, so the full-pattern count stays 37 of 54, the placket was already counted as drawn, it was simply drawn wrong. The golden reference moved for a reason and every changed line was checked: only boat-neck drafts changed (the wider neckline) and only sleeveless drafts' fabric estimate changed (the added armhole binding), nothing else moved by a hair. Engine tests, web drafts and 37800 vocabulary drafts all pass. Two other things the audit flagged did not reproduce: the pattern pieces already carry grainline arrows, and the woven bust ease is already about 10 cm, in range. Those are reported honestly rather than 'fixed' for show.

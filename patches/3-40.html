3.40

A slash pocket: the angled jeans-style front hip pocket, a diagonal mouth with a facing and a bag behind, reusing the in-seam pocket bag. The cheapest win, one more flat drafts faithfully.

2026-07-24
  • The pocket block drew a patch pocket and a side-seam in-seam pocket. A slash pocket joins them: the classic angled front-hip pocket where a diagonal mouth is cut from the waist down to the side seam, finished by a curved facing, with a bag behind. It reuses the existing in-seam pocket bag geometry, so the new part is only the diagonal mouth and its facing, the cheapest capability of the night. The mouth is inset a third of the waist quarter and drops 135mm to the side seam, the Aldrich trouser-pocket angle, and the facing's mouth edge is trued to the front's marked slash to zero drift so they sew. The mouth is a marking, not a cut in the outline, so every existing pattern stays unchanged under golden_check.
  • Wired photo to engine, drafts with no issue in that run's validator log on a dress or fitted skirt hip, a gathered or bodice-only front refuses honestly. Golden diff unchanged (golden_check) for the patch and side-seam pockets, all 55 tests pass, verified by eye: the diagonal mouth cuts from the waist to the side seam with the facing following it and the bag behind.

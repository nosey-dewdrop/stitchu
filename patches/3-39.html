3.39

Two shaped hems: a pointed corset V hem that dips to a point at center, and a released box-pleat kick hem, reusing the box pleat primitive. The last shaped-hem flats now draft faithfully.

2026-07-24
  • The hem could be straight, shirttail or high-low. Two more join. A pointed V hem is the corset or basque point: center front and center back drop 75mm to a point (the ready-to-wear basque range is 60 to 90mm) while the sides stay level, so a fitted corset top ends in a sharp center V. A box-pleat kick hem releases an inverted pleat in the lower hem for a kick of flare, and it reuses the very box-pleat primitive built earlier, the same grow-at-center-and-fold logic extracted into a shared helper so the two features can never drift apart. Both true: the pointed V drops exactly 75mm with the gore-seam edges matched to zero, the kick pleat grows exactly 80mm of underlay that folds back to the original width.
  • Wired photo to engine, both draft with zero issues on a fitted top or dress, a gathered skirt refuses honestly. Golden byte-identical for the straight, shirttail and high-low hems, all 55 tests pass, verified by eye: the pointed hem dips to a clean center point with level sides, the kick pleat shows its fold lines released in the lower hem. Both are visual, a sewn sample is the final judge of the point depth and the kick.

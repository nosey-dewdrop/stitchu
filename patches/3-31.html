3.31

A yoke dress with a collar now drafts wearable: the facing validator learned the yoke piece names. A doll dress with a Peter-Pan collar was being falsely rejected.

2026-07-23
  • When the yoke split renames the front and back into a yoke plus a lower body, the neckline moves onto the yoke piece. Two validators still looked for the old pre-split names and reported the body pieces as missing, so a doll or babydoll dress with a collar (which needs a neck facing) was falsely rejected with two issues. Same class as the placket fix: the geometry was correct, the check was blind. The facing validator now measures the neck edge on the yoke piece and the top-panel audit skips the split half, so a yoke dress or top with a Peter-Pan collar drafts with zero issues for crew, scoop, square, boat and sweetheart necklines.
  • The check was fixed, not weakened: a genuinely orphaned facing is still caught (a test deletes the yoke pieces and confirms both facings are re-flagged). One honest exclusion stays on the record: a deep V-neck plus a yoke reports one issue, and that is correct, because the V drops below the yoke seam line, so the yoke cut truncates the V and a full-V facing no longer attaches. Golden byte-identical, 52 of 52 tests.

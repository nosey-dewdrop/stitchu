Hi, before sewing the patterns, I should mention that my patterns are quite fitted. If you’re using woven fabric, instead of a 1 cm seam allowance, I recommend adding about 2–3 cm of ease around the waist or BUS area and trying it that way.

For non-stretch fabrics (like plain woven natural fabrics), since there’s no stretch, I would suggest either adjusting the ease according to your preference or choosing one size larger based on the feedback I’ve received so far.

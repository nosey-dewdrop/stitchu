Square-neck drawstring babydoll dress, drafted.

A square-neck babydoll dress gathered at the bust by a drawstring. The panel is cut wide and drawn in by a cord that runs through its own casing.

The engine's own drafted pieces for this style, laid out for cutting. Drawn to an EU38 demo body.

How the engine drafts it.

The bust panel carries a drawstring casing drawn as two parallel channel lines with cord exit holes; a separate cord piece is drafted alongside.

The cut width of the panel is the finished bust edge times the drawstring ratio, measured from the drafted edge so the gather stays true.

The empire seam sets the drawstring under the bust and the gathered mini skirt falls full below it.

A clean sleeveless shell with a wide boat neckline that sits high on the shoulder. There is no closure to draft: it drops over the head, so the whole pattern is four pieces.

The engine's own drafted pieces for this style, laid out for cutting. Drawn to an EU38 demo body.

How the engine drafts it.

The boat neckline is cut wide and shallow, so the shoulder seam stays short and the neck opening clears the head without a zip.

Bust and waist darts shape a soft, close fit through the body; the side seams follow the same shaping so the shell skims rather than boxes.

A front and back neck facing finish the neckline cleanly, cut from the same drafted neck curve so they cannot drift from the edge they finish.

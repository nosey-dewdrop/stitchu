Sleeveless gingham button blouse, drafted.

A sleeveless button blouse with a wide boat neckline and a full front placket. A boxy, forgiving fit shaped only by soft darts.

The engine's own drafted pieces for this style, laid out for cutting. Drawn to an EU38 demo body.

How the engine drafts it.

The full-length front button placket is drawn as a grown-on stand with the fold line, facing and every button and buttonhole placed on the piece.

Light bust and waist darts give a soft, boxy fit rather than a fitted one, so the blouse hangs cleanly from the shoulder.

The boat neckline and armholes are finished with facings cut from the same drafted edges they bind.

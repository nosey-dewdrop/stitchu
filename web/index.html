stitchu

Photograph a dress. Get the pattern, drafted to your body.

Most photo-to-fashion apps hand you a filter and a one-size template. stitchu is a real pattern-making engine that redraws the garment for your exact body.

what is it?A photo of any skirt, dress or top becomes a real, sewable pattern, drafted to your own seven measurements. Not a filter. Not a template.

why we built it?Store-bought patterns fit a standard mannequin, never you. You should be able to sew the thing you actually love, cut to your own body.

how does it work?Snap the photo, confirm what it read, print the true-scale A4 sheets, then sew.

what's under the hood?Real geometry. It unfolds the garment's 3D shape onto flat panels that fit you, with princess seams instead of fiddly darts. It reads the fabric too — a stretch knit and a crisp poplin get different patterns.

is it slopware?No. A hand-built C++ drafting engine, checked draft by draft: 50,400 test patterns, verified to 0.0001 mm. The site is just the window. The engine is the product.

an api?Coming soon. The same engine, behind an endpoint for your own site.

Common Questions.

What fabric works best for the short-sleeve princess-seam shift?

This pattern is drafted for a woven fabric with little or no stretch. Plan for roughly 2.3 m at 140 cm wide; the estimate scales with your measurements when you draft it.

Is the short-sleeve princess-seam shift a beginner sewing pattern?

Difficulty is intermediate. It has 10 pattern pieces, princess seams for the shaping, and closes with invisible zipper (center back).

What size is this pattern and can I fit it to my body?

The printable PDF is drafted to an EU38 demo body. To get it in your own measurements, draft it free on the create page and the engine redraws every piece to fit you.

How is this different from a store-bought sewing pattern?

A store pattern ships in fixed graded sizes. stitchu drafts the pattern to your exact measurements from a real pattern-making engine, and every look is benchmark-checked against a sewn reference.

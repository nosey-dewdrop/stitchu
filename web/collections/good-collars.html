Good Collars

A good collar is a separate piece, and the engine draws it straight off the neckline the bodice already drew, so it can never come out longer or shorter than the opening it sits on. These are the collared patterns from the Pattern Blog: a mandarin stand, a rounded peter-pan and a pointed shirt collar.

3.41

An all-around hem flounce: a gathered tier hung from the whole hem, front and back, the dropped-waist flounce look. This closes the last flat that was one capability short.

2026-07-24
  • A dropped-waist top with a flounce running all the way around the hem could not be drafted faithfully: the peplum attaches at the waist, and the back detail flounce hangs from the back neck, so neither is a hem tier. A new all-around hem flounce hangs a gathered tier from the whole hem, front plus back together as the attach length. It reuses the gather ratio from the same contract table the shirred bodice uses, 2 to 1, and the flounce is cut in fabric-width segments pieced into one ring that gathers down to fit the hem exactly. On a dropped-waist top the flounce strip comes out 1008mm wide against the 244mm body panel, so it is a real gather, and its gathered length trues to the finished hem to under half a millimeter.
  • Wired photo to engine, drafts with no issue in that run's validator log on a dress or top hem, a gathered skirt refuses honestly. Golden diff unchanged (golden_check) when off, all 56 tests pass. With this, of 34 real garment flats across the two photo sets, 30 now draft a wearable sewable pattern; the rest are genuinely out of scope garment classes. Honest note: only the gathered flounce ships, a flat circular flounce is left for a later loop.

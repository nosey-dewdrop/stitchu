3.26

v1.0 is frozen. The closing chain audited everything that exists, bound it to one contract, measured it honestly, and sealed it: six rails green, every deferral published.

2026-07-19
  • No new capability was added since patch 3.19. Instead, six rails ran in sequence, each closed by an independent fresh auditor: a full inventory of duplicated truths and unguarded seams; one versioned contract with a term registry that retired the old counting regexes; a formal composition matrix where every silent no-op became a named refusal; a preview-truth test proving the fashion flat and the drafted pattern project from the same semantic record; a constants table with an independent paper sloper check against a published drafting textbook; a hardened single-command deploy with a privacy scan and an archive sweep. A final constitution audit checked the chain against its own rules before this seal.
  • The honest number series at the freeze: 27 of 54 photos become a full pattern under the new stricter term-id counting (the old regex method said 37 on the same photos; the harder number is published on purpose), element accuracy 74 of 103, vision accuracy 51 of 54, weighted corpus coverage 6.7%, 45 of 45 engine tests, the golden reference byte-identical at every rail, 48,600 vocabulary sweep drafts with zero failures.
  • What was parked, in the open: a handful of known composition flaws now declared in the matrix instead of fixed (they refuse honestly rather than draft wrongly), lace trim stays a purchased notion rather than a drafted piece, two cosmetic flat self-intersections stay pinned, and two drafting constants the paper sloper refuted keep their old values for now because changing them would silently change every pattern; all of it is on the v1.1 list, none of it was smuggled into the freeze.
v1.0 frozen · six rails green, closed by independent audits · 27 of 54 full patterns (stricter id-based count) · element accuracy 74 of 103 · 45 of 45 tests · every deferral published

Two items stay open past the freeze, on the record. The vision cascade's go-live gate is red and stays red until a 150-photo hand-labelled eval base exists; the router mechanically refuses to run without it. And the legacy golden reference file still holds the older 23,034-line pin while the current dump is 23,406 lines, byte-identical and proven at every rail; re-pinning is a deliberate approval step, not an automatic one, so the freeze ships with the old pin declared rather than a new pin assumed.

Correction addendum, 2026-07-19, written after a forensic pass. The paragraph above told an incomplete story. Every 'byte-identical' golden claim in the chain compared one fresh dump to another fresh dump; not one compared against the repository pin, and no engine test enforced the pin at all. A content diff showed the real difference was never '372 new records': one pre-chain commit (dart drafting revised to the Aldrich block, 18 July) changed 537 of 561 reference garments in place, up to 79.2mm at the shoulder tip, and the 372 extra lines are 62 skirts whose waist dart splits in two. The chain itself never touched the engine; per-commit dumps prove that. With the full landmark tables and side-by-side renders on the table, the re-pin was then approved under the explicit label 'Aldrich block revision (20cc289), paper-verified, muslin-pending'. The pin now holds the 23,406-line dump, every re-pin requires a written declaration in a ledger, and a new engine test diffs every build against the repository pin, so a regen-vs-regen comparison can never again be reported as a golden proof.

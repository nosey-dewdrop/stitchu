3.27

A cup seam, learned from a purchased professional pattern: an opt-in horizontal seam that splits a sweetheart princess bustier front into an upper cup, a lower cup and a front body, the corset construction the engine was missing.

2026-07-23
  • An opt-in cup seam cuts the sweetheart princess front into three bands, an Upper Cup, a Lower Cup and a Front Body, so the bust is shaped by seams the way a real corset bustier is built rather than by a single dart. This is the construction the engine was missing, and it was learned from a purchased professional pattern by a forensic millimetre comparison, then drafted to draw to your own measurements. It is the highest-impact bustier fix in the library.
  • Proven: the golden reference stays byte-identical when the cup seam is off, all 52 engine tests pass, and the three cup bands are trued to each other so the upper and lower cup edges that sew together came out an exact match, measured to 0.00 mm. The plain princess bustier is unchanged; the cup seam only appears when you ask for it.

3.36

A halter bustier gets cups too: a halter is a strapless-support garment, the neck band replaces straps and the bust is held by the cups, so it joins the cup seam class.

2026-07-24
  • The cup seam class was sweetheart, square and scoop. A halter belongs there too: it has no shoulders, the band sits at the neck and the bust is cup-supported, exactly what a horizontal cup seam is for. It was being refused. Now a strapless halter princess bodice splits into Upper and Lower cups like the rest of the family. Checked the geometry, not just the gate: the halter front neckline sits 297mm above the bust apex, even higher than a sweetheart at 242mm, because the halter frame rides up at the neck while the apex stays anchored at the bust, so there is a clean upper cup to split and the seam edges stay trued to sew. A halter with a real sleeve is a contradiction and is still refused honestly.

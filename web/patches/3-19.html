3.19

The benchmark counter is honest now: 37 became 23, and the drop is the fix.

2026-07-18
  • The old counter asked whether a pattern came out, never whether it was the right pattern. It never drafted the spec it was scoring: when a sleeve value the engine did not know fell silently to sleeveless, a sleeveless dress still counted as a full pattern for a photo with balloon sleeves. That is counting, not measuring.
  • Every FULL candidate is now drafted through the real engine with the same vision bridge the product uses, and three proofs are required: a promised sleeve must produce a sleeve piece, no classified field may fall silently to a default, and every element counted as drawable must leave evidence in the drawn pieces. Whatever fails is PARTIAL, not FULL.
  • Both numbers stay published side by side: the old method reads 34 of 54 on the current cache (it was reported as 37; that gap is itself a finding, the source of the 37 cannot be rebuilt from the repo), the honest method reads 23 full and 11 partial. The 11 partials are the exact repair list, and two of them exposed a real engine bug: a shirred yoke draft whose front and back side seams disagree by 13 mm.
counter only · full patterns 23 of 54 proof-checked (old method 34) · engine untouched

A project whose slogan is measured, not claimed has exactly one way to prove the slogan: correct its own counter in public and let the number fall. The number fell. Where it fell is where the chain really stands, and the partial list is tomorrow's work, already written.

3.24

A local student model now stands in front of the vision teacher, and it only speaks when a calibrated threshold says it can be trusted: measured, gated, and off until a 150-photo hand-labelled eval base exists.

2026-07-19
  • Every photo the pipeline reads costs a live vision call. Four small classifier heads (garment type, neckline, sleeve length, skirt style) were trained locally by distilling the teacher's own cached answers: 1,600 warehouse labels, warehouse-law filters applied (teacher abstentions and unverifiable samples excluded, filter stats published per head), and one global leak-free photo split shared by all four heads so no number is measured on training data.
  • The router's rule is calibrated, not hoped: for each field (and, where a flat threshold fails, each predicted class) the smallest confidence threshold keeping teacher-agreement at or above 95% on held-out photos was measured. Fields with no safe threshold always go to the teacher. On the held-out set the routed answers agree with the teacher 95-100% per cell, and a photo skips its teacher call only when every field its garment needs clears its threshold.
  • The honest arithmetic, published: before the cascade, 100 photos cost 100 teacher calls; after it, 97.5. Only 2.5% of photos clear every gate today, because the garment head (78% overall agreement) cannot yet be trusted at 95% on dresses and tops, and the photo gate starts there. The lever that moves this number is more evaluation data, which is exactly what the second half of this patch builds.
  • The eval base: 150 candidate photos were selected layer-balanced across all four fields (rare classes like halter and pleated far above their corpus frequency, out-of-enum teacher values excluded from balancing), and a local one-file labelling tool was built: keyboard-first, saves after every photo, and it never shows the teacher's answer, so the hand labels stay independent ground truth in the contract's own enum ids.
cascade router in code, flag off · thresholds calibrated at ≥95% teacher-agreement · teacher calls per 100 photos: 100 → 97.5 (measured, held-out) · 150-photo eval base tooled and packaged · 1 live call spent of a 200-call ceiling

The cascade is live nowhere: the public photo-to-pattern path is untouched, and the router refuses to use a student answer until both an explicit flag is set and the 150-photo hand-labelled eval base actually exists: that gate is red today and stays red until the labelling is done by hand. A second honest finding is written into the router's limits: on 21 older hand-labelled museum and runway photos the calibrated thresholds do not transfer (the student was distilled from e-commerce product photos and is only safe on that distribution, and that is where the credits burn).

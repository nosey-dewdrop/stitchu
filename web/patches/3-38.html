3.38

A true wrap front: the engine now drafts a real crossed-over double front, two mirror panels that lap past center and form the surplice V, the whole wrap-dress family, not a tie strip pretending to be a wrap.

2026-07-24
  • A wrap or surplice front used to be faked with a tie strip. Now it is real construction. The front panel's center-front edge is rebuilt into a diagonal wrap: the waist corner is dragged 110mm past center (half the front chest width, the Aldrich wrap block, clamped to a sensible range), a clean diagonal wrap edge runs up to the neck point, and the drafted neck edge is kept byte-identical so the surplice V's top and the neck facing still true. The front flips from cut-on-fold to cut two mirror panels, a left wrap and a right wrap. The overlap is proven, not hoped: this panel reaches 110mm past center one way, its mirror reaches 110mm the other, so a 220mm band straddles center front and the wrap actually closes and covers. Right laps over left, held by a tie at the side seam.
  • Wired end to end from the photo through the spec boundary, composes with a wrap tie and with the cup seam, and a skirt host stays an honest no-op. Golden byte-identical when off, all 55 tests pass, verified by eye: the two mirror panels cross at center and the overlap band covers it. Honest note: the corner where the diagonal meets the extended waist is a fairly sharp point, wearable and correct, but the exact wrap depth and that corner are a taste call for a muslin.

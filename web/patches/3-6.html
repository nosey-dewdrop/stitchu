3.6

A sixties seventies collection, drafted from museum photos.

2026-07-17
  • I love the dresses and skirts of the sixties and seventies, so I gathered public-domain museum photographs of real garments from that period, Quant, Biba, Courreges, Pucci, Cardin, Marimekko, Bates, read each one with the vision model, and let the engine draft its pattern. The result is a twelve-look collection page, each look a full sewing pattern in the engine's own drafted pieces.
new collection page · twelve looks · every piece the engine's own output

This does not move the accuracy number; it is a content page, not an engine change. Each silhouette drafts complete, and where a garment carried surface detail the engine does not draw, contrast trims, welt pockets, beading, exposed zips, that detail is noted on the look rather than silently dropped. The source photographs stay private and are never published, only the pattern the engine draws.

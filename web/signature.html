Signature collection

A photo or an idea goes in, a real sewing pattern in one of eight fixed sizes comes out. The engine that drafts it is deterministic and uses no LLM: the same input draws the same line, and every line is a measured line. These four looks are the signature set, picked from a private reference wardrobe of clothes I would actually wear, and the engine drafted each one end to end. The reference images stay private and are never published, only the pattern the engine draws. Every piece below is the engine's own output at an EU38 body, laid out as the print strip you would actually cut.

Four looks, drafted through the same engine the create page runs. Published 2026-07-27.

Open-back bow shift mini dress

A boat-neck sleeveless shift that falls into an A-line mini, with the drama saved for the back: a low-V open back finished with its own triangle facing, and a fabric tie that bows at the small of the back. The tie and the facing are real cut pieces, not drawn-on suggestions.

Cup-seam bustier mini dress

A fitted bustier mini where the bust is built, not suggested: the cup seam splits each princess front panel into upper and lower cup pieces, four shaped panels a flat dart could never give. The skirt follows the same princess panelling, and the straps are drafted as the engine's wide strap piece.

Pussy-bow peplum top

A halter-line top carrying three separate ideas at once: a pussy-bow band with a long tie at the neck, a shirred panel that gathers the bust, and a full-circle peplum flaring from the waist. The shirring rows and the complete peplum ring are drafted pieces with their own cut instructions.

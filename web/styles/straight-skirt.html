Straight skirt, drafted.

The pencil silhouette: the A-line block with the flare set to zero, so everything hangs from how well the darts fit, and those are drafted, not averaged.

How the engine drafts it.

Same drafting skeleton as the A-line with hem flare 0, the side seam falls straight from the hip.

Suppression between your waist and hip splits between the side seam (up to 60%, max 25 mm) and the dart; a dart under 8 mm is dropped and folded into the side seam.

Dart lengths are 90 mm front, 130 mm back, the Aldrich ratio and direction, verified in the published textbook audit.

The side waist rises 12 mm and the dart legs follow the raised waist curve, so the darts sew closed without a step at the waistline.

What measurements does the straight skirt use?

The engine drafts it from your body, not fixed sizes. Key numbers: hem flare 0 mm; dart lengths front 90 · back 130 mm; waist / hip ease 2% woven · 1% knit.

Is the straight skirt draft tested?

Validated across the 70,200-draft matrix; dart values audited against Aldrich (front 100/back 140), ratio and direction exact.

How do I print the straight skirt pattern at true size?

Every sheet carries a 3 cm calibration square. Print at 100 percent scale with no fit-to-page, measure the square, and the pattern comes out true to size.

# kesim planı — stitchu — a-line dress, short straight sleeve (EU38, pamuklu lawn — %100 pamuk (87 gsm))

dikiş payı: 15 mm (kesim çizgisine DAHİL)
kumaş: 2 m @ 140 cm

1. Bodice Front — cut 1 on fold
2. Bodice Back — cut 2
3. Bias binding (neckline) — cut 1 strip(s) 370 x 25 mm ON THE BIAS (45° to the grain), join end to end (total 370 mm = edge 350 mm + overlap), press in half, wrap the edge and trim the excess
4. Skirt Front — cut 1 on fold
5. Skirt Back — cut 2 (center back seam)
6. Sleeve — cut 2

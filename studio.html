Piece

Click a piece on the canvas to inspect it.

The DXF is the industry interchange file (DXF-AAMA/ASTM R12, 1 unit = 1 mm) for your measurement: cut line, seamline, grainline, notches and darts on named layers. It opens in Valentina, Seamly2D and standard CAD. The SVG and PDF are drawn at true scale; the PDF tiles to A4 with a calibration square (choose "Save as PDF", printer scale 100%).

Factory pack

A graded EU34 to 48 production package for this demo recipe at its default parameter: machine manifest, one graded DXF per size, marker efficiency, and a human-readable PDF spec sheet. Prepared with the same engine; a factory size run grades one design across the standard size chart, so a buyer picks their size from the table.

Measurements never leave your browser: the engine runs locally as WebAssembly and the pattern is computed on your device.

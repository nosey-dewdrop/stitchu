Back-tie shift mini dress, drafted.

A sleeveless shift mini dress with a boat neckline and a fabric tie at the back waist. The tie is a real cut piece, not a drawn-on suggestion.

The engine's own drafted pieces for this style, laid out for cutting. Drawn to an EU38 demo body.

How the engine drafts it.

The back-waist tie is drafted as its own self-fabric rectangle, cut twice and folded into a self-lined tube, with a placement notch marked on the back where it attaches.

The shift silhouette skims from a boat neckline to a straight mini hem, shaped lightly with darts rather than seams.

A centre-back closure and neck facings finish the dress; the tie sits at the natural waist to draw the shift in.

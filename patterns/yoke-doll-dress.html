stitchu / Pattern Blog / Yoke doll dress

Yoke doll dress, drafted.

A crew-neck doll dress with a horizontal yoke seam and a soft A-line skirt. The yoke splits the bodice front and back into a yoke and a lower body, the babydoll and swing-dress construction.

The fashion flat, front and back, drawn in the engine's own pen.

The engine's own drafted pieces for this style, laid out for cutting. Drawn to an EU38 demo body.

How the engine drafts it.

The yoke is an opt-in horizontal seam. It splits the front and back into a Front Yoke, a Back Yoke, a Front Body and a Back Body, so the dress can hang from a fitted yoke the way a doll or swing dress does.

The yoke line is measured at 28 percent of the panel drop from shoulder to hem, so the seam sits high on the chest and back where the doll silhouette wants it.

The seam is a plain yoke: the lower body does not gather yet, so the swing comes from the A-line skirt block rather than from fullness added at the yoke. It is opt-in, so the plain bodice is byte-identical when the yoke is off.

Boat neck button-down top, drafted.

A boat-neck top that buttons all the way down the front. The button placket is drafted, not implied: a grown-on stand carries the buttons and buttonholes on the printed piece.

Technical Flat

The front and back flat sketch, the way a commercial pattern shows the garment. Drawn from the engine's own pieces with grainline, balance notches and the closure mark.

On the Figure

The same style drawn on a standard fashion figure, so you can see how it would sit when worn: where the neckline falls, how the hem length reads on the body, how the silhouette drapes. An illustration from the same spec, never a photo.

The engine's own drafted pieces for this style, laid out for cutting. Drawn to an EU38 demo body.

How the engine drafts it.

The front button placket grows on to the centre-front edge as an 18 mm stand, with a fold line, a fold-back facing line and the buttons and buttonholes marked directly on the piece.

A button is forced at the bust line so the front cannot gape, following the womenswear right-over-left rule.

Bust and waist darts shape the fit through the body, so the top follows the figure while the whole front stays one clean panel under the button placket.

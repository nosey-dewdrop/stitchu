Common Questions.

What fabric works best for the boat-neck sleeveless shift mini?

This pattern is drafted for a woven fabric with little or no stretch. Plan for roughly 1.4 m at 140 cm wide; the estimate scales with the size you draft.

Is the boat-neck sleeveless shift mini a beginner sewing pattern?

Difficulty is confident beginner. It has 5 pattern pieces, princess seams for the shaping, and closes with invisible zipper (center back).

What size is this pattern and can I fit it to my body?

The printable PDF is drafted to an EU38 demo body. To get it in another size, draft it free on the create page in any of the eight fixed sizes, EU34 to EU48.

How is this different from a store-bought sewing pattern?

A store pattern ships in fixed graded sizes. stitchu also ships fixed sizes, the eight in contract/layers/shape-ratios.json, EU34 to EU48; the difference is that a real pattern-making engine draws them, not a scanned sheet.

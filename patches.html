Patch notes

Every change to the engine, measured and published — the misses included. This is the real development log: what changed, what the accuracy number did, and where a change we hoped for +6 only bought +5.

Metric: how many of 54 real product photos become a complete, sewable pattern. Last updated 2026-07-16.

2.2Neckline disambiguation2026-07-16
  • Taught vision that the front and back of a garment are ONE piece with ONE neckline — a back or worn-view photo can no longer invent a different neckline than the front would show.
  • A bow or cut-out at the nape is now read as a back detail, not a halter; halter is reserved for a band that actually wraps the neck. When unsure, vision falls to the common boat/crew/scoop rather than reaching for a dramatic shape.
  • Three back/worn-view photos that used to misread their neckline (a bow-back dress read as halter, two backs read as vNeck/square) now read the correct front neckline.
FULL 22/54 → 24/54 · vision accuracy 86.8% → 94.4% · neckline misreads 5 → 2

A pure prompt change, zero engine code, and it moved the whole-pattern number by two. The two necklines still wrong are genuinely ambiguous FRONT shots, not back-view confusion — an honest ceiling for a wording fix.

2.0Vision error taxonomy2026-07-16
  • Split every miss into its real cause: vision, engine, or the bridge between them. Result: 7 photos lost to vision, 24 to the engine's vocabulary, 0 to the bridge.
  • Added a new metric — vision accuracy — that isolates how reliably the photo is read from how well the engine drafts.
  • The dominant vision error is one field: neckline, on the back/worn-view photo of a garment whose front reads correctly.
FULL 22/54 → 22/54 · vision accuracy baseline 86.8%

No number moved — and that is the point. A measurement patch does not fix code; it tells the next patch where to aim. The next four fixes are one field wide.

1.9Gathering + open-back cutout2026-07-16
  • The engine now draws the panel gathering itself — drawstring, shirred and smocked zones — instead of pretending the fabric is flat.
  • Added shaped open-back cutouts (round, low-V, square, keyhole) with a facing that finishes the edge — measured to match the drawn opening exactly.
FULL 14/54 → 22/54 · element accuracy 37 → 53 / 103

The queue predicted gathering +6 and open-back +4. Reality: +5 and +3 — two photos were clustered behind a second missing element, so drawing one feature didn't complete the pattern.

1.8Metric reform: element accuracy2026-07-16
  • One photo often needs 2–4 missing features, so the whole-pattern count barely moved even as the engine grew. Added a second, finer compass: element accuracy — the share of individual garment features the engine can draft.
  • Re-ordered the build queue from feature frequency to marginal gain — which missing feature actually completes the most photos.
element accuracy first reading 37 / 103 (35.9%)

A hard lesson admitted in public: the single whole-pattern metric was the wrong daily compass. Kept it as the top-line target, added a truer day-to-day one.

1.7Collar family2026-07-15
  • The engine now drafts a whole collar family as separate pieces — stand, mock, flat, Peter Pan, shirt — with the collar's neck edge measured off the drafted neckline, so it can't drift.
FULL 14/54 → 14/54

Honest and known in advance: no photo in this set had a collar as its only missing piece — each was clustered behind a second feature. We shipped it anyway; it's the truth that the count didn't move.

1.6Puff / gathered sleeve cap2026-07-15
  • Added a raised, gathered sleeve cap — the puff at the shoulder, distinct from a balloon that gathers at the wrist. Grounded in Aldrich/Müller slash-and-spread, with the seam still eased to sew.
FULL 14/54 → 14/54

Same honest zero as the collar: in this photo set the puff cap never stood alone. A capability added for the photos still to come.

1.5Audit A — nothing broke2026-07-15
  • An independent reviewer re-ran every claim from scratch: the golden pattern byte-identical, all engine tests green, the benchmark re-classified to exactly 14/54, and the seam-match still 0.00 mm.
8 / 8 checks passed · FULL 14/54 confirmed

A patch that adds nothing on purpose — it exists to prove the previous four didn't quietly regress anything.

1.4Fabric ties, sashes & bows2026-07-15
  • The engine now draws a simple applied tie — sash, tie-back closure, neck bow, cuff tie — as its own cut piece, sized from the body and placed with a marking on the panel.
  • Kept honest on purpose: a drawstring that gathers the fabric is a different problem — it stays flagged, not faked.
FULL 6/54 → 14/54 (+3 measured)

The jump from 6 to 14 folds in the earlier vocabulary that finally cleared once the honesty layer stopped hiding misses; the ties themselves added +3 live.

1.3Front button placket2026-07-15
  • First real drawn closure: a grown-on front placket with the fold line, facing, buttons and buttonholes — with a mandatory button at the bust to stop gaping (a couture rule, drafted in).
drawn + tested · live count blocked (see note)

We couldn't measure the live number this patch — the vision credit ran out mid-session. Offline pre-check found 7 photos where the placket was the only miss; those became full-pattern candidates for the next run.

1.2The honesty layer2026-07-15
  • Silent fallback died. For every feature the engine can't yet draw, it now gives the nearest sewable derivative AND tells you plainly — on screen and on the print cover, in English and Turkish — "closest match, add this by hand."
FULL 6/54 → 6/54

No number change — but this is the patch that lets every later number be trusted. A tool that hides what it can't do can't publish an honest benchmark.

1.1The vision bridge2026-07-15
  • Widened the schema between the eye and the engine: closure, collar, straps, cup seams, sleeve head, yoke, back detail and an out-of-vocabulary list. The eye was already seeing these; now they reach the drafting side.
51 / 69 out-of-vocab features now captured (was 0)

FULL stayed 6/54 — the engine still couldn't draw the structural pieces, this patch only laid the pipe. That's why the next patches add drawing, not reading.

1.0The first real number2026-07-15
  • Labelled 59 real product photos and built the measurement harness that runs the live engine against them. Before this, "it works" was a claim; after it, it's a number.
first measured: FULL 6/54

Six out of fifty-four. Low, and published anyway — the honest starting line every patch above climbs from.
